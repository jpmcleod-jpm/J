Give your drums somewhere to go.

Upload a single 4/4 drum MIDI clip. Add a full-song audio bounce to guide fills toward changes in the song’s energy. Your original notes stay unchanged; new hits use your chosen drum pads.

Export the drum clip from Ableton using Export MIDI Clip. All processing stays on your device.

Match your Ableton Drum Rack pads

MIDI pitch numbers must match the pads in your rack. Defaults use common General MIDI assignments. Ableton’s octave labels differ; use the pitch number or compare with the original note list below.

This version uses groove rules and audio energy analysis, not a trained AI model. Energy changes can suggest transitions, but do not identify verses, choruses, or genre. Check alignment and audition the MIDI in Ableton.

BUILD THE BALANCE

Give every part its place.

Upload separate vocal, drum, bass, and instrument audio stems from Ableton. Export every stem from the same start point, keeping leading silence. These files play together; the app does not automatically align recordings or separate a finished song.

Up to 160 MB combined, five minutes per stem, and 64 million decoded samples total. Shorter bounces work best on phones. Files stay on your device and are not saved when you leave.

Choose all your stems together. Selecting again replaces this set.

Listen to the arrangement

Preview is attenuated to leave headroom. Original balance means the uploaded stems at unity gain, centered, with mute/solo bypassed. This is not your Ableton master bus. Compare at similar perceived volume.

Suggestions use measured average levels, not an AI mixing model. Sparse instruments and artistic balance still need your judgment. Export includes your levels, pans, mute and solo settings—not the preview volume or comparison selection. The existing stereo mix helper above can polish the combined bounce.

FIND YOUR SINGING KEY

Make the song fit your voice.

Sing comfortable notes, without stretching or straining. Use a quiet room with no instruments playing. Microphone audio is analyzed on your device and is never uploaded or saved.

Choose your comfortable range manually, or use your microphone over HTTPS.

Have a melody already? Get a closer fit.

Set the melody’s current key and its lowest and highest notes. Or sing your entire verse and chorus to estimate those notes. Include the highest part of the song. This measures pitch range; it does not identify the melody’s key. Select the melody’s scale/mode in the main controls; suggested transpositions keep that mode.
